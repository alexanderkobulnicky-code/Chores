The task may be completed at any time during the week. It is Due Today on the day before this setting. If you miss the weekly window, it becomes Overdue when the new week begins.

Example: Maximum 7 days and Pending window 2 days. If completed Monday, it remains Done through Saturday, is Pending Sunday, is Due Today the following Monday, and becomes Overdue Tuesday.

Daily tasks have no timing settings. If completed today they are Done. If completed yesterday they are Pending. Otherwise they are Overdue.
