Add task

A completed weekly task remains Done until the next “Resets to Pending” day. The day immediately before “Becomes Overdue” is Due Today.

Example: Maximum 7 days and Pending window 2 days. If completed Monday, it remains Done through Saturday, is Pending Sunday, is Due Today the following Monday, and becomes Overdue Tuesday.

Each ＋ records one occurrence. The counter resets at the start of each week. If the target is still unfinished on the final day, it becomes Due Today. A missed week breaks the streak, but the new week begins fresh instead of carrying an Overdue debt forward.

Daily tasks are Due Today until completed. If a whole day passes without completion, they become Overdue.
Planned pauses

A new pause must begin at least 7 days from today. Paused days do not count toward or against a streak. Planned pauses do not spoil a Clean Sweep. For a Times-per-Week task, the target does not shrink for individual paused days. The whole week is excused only when a planned pause covers the entire weekly window.

Add to-do

Before “Can start,” the item is Pending. On that date it becomes To Do. On its Due by date it is Due Today; the following day it becomes Overdue. Leave either date blank if it does not apply.

Emergency Day

An Emergency Day excuses recurring chores without pretending they were completed. Streaks freeze, the day is marked Excused on the calendar, and it cannot count as a Clean Sweep or Perfect Week. To Do items are unaffected.
